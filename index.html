Who we are

Forty-two neighbours and one granite tower

We are retired harbour pilots, a welder, two schoolteachers and the owner of the ferry café. When the last keeper left in 1988, the lamp went automatic and the tower was left to the weather. We formed the trust to look after it.

Why we exist

The ledge is still out there

Merrow Ledge lies 900 metres offshore and disappears at high tide. Fishing boats cross this water before dawn, and satellites sometimes fail. The light tells a skipper whether the screen is right.
